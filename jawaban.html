Strategi ujian ML&P (Hoedt)

Email Hoedt (21 Nov 2026): “The ML exam aims to test everything you know about ML. It is up to you to figure out which topics to study in depth.” Artinya: ujian bidang, bukan satu mata kuliah. Beliau tidak membatasi topik, dan memilih yang penting termasuk kompetensi. Laporan S29 dan T1: mulai dari fondasi, lalu backprop dan $\delta^{[l]}$, lalu optimisasi; gaya Socratic (definisi lalu sebab-akibat).

Prioritas: (1) A1–A3, A6, A8–A9, B1–B4 · (2) bab tulisan Hoedt: A10–A13, B5, F1–F7 · (3) C1–C4, A4–A5, D1–D4.

Topik pembuka (kalau ditanya “Where would you like to start?”): “I'd like to start with how a neural network learns: empirical risk minimization and backpropagation.”

Bridging (sambungkan ke topik yang dikuasai):

  • “…and this recursion is also where vanishing gradients come from, which is why initialization matters.”
  • “Adam improves on this by adapting the step size per weight. I can write the update if you like.”
  • “For a linear model I can derive the closed form and show the link to maximum likelihood.”

Kalau tidak tahu: “I'm not sure about the details, but from the basics I would reason like this…” atau “I don't remember the exact formula, but the idea is…”

Hindari: “I only prepared X and Y”; menulis rumus dengan yakin padahal ragu. Jangan kirim daftar topik ke Hoedt.
